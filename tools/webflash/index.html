Flash a ground station modem

No toolchain, no PlatformIO. A browser, a USB cable, and the board.

Before you start
  1. Use Chrome or Edge — Web Serial does not exist in Safari or Firefox.
  2. Plug the modem into USB. Nothing else needs to be running: close the RaptorHAB app first, or it will hold the port.
  3. If the board is not offered when you click Connect, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, and try again.
